# Humans vs Zombies

A 3D lane-defense game that runs in the browser. Place riflemen, oil drillers, landmines and barbed wire on a 9×9 lawn to keep waves of zombies out of the camp.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing needs to be installed or built. The page loads Three.js (r128) from cdnjs and its fonts from Google Fonts, so it needs an internet connection.

To host it, turn on GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).

## How to play

- **Rifleman ($100):** shoots down his lane and switches to a knife when a zombie gets close.
- **Driller ($50):** pumps oil and earns $15 about every 6 seconds.
- **Mine ($75):** a builder runs out of the tent and plants it. It blows up the first zombie that steps on it.
- **Wire ($50 per square):** drag a straight line. It slows zombies down and wears them out.
- Coneheads show up from wave 3 and soak up extra damage.
- Difficulty and a low-detail grass option are in **Settings**.
